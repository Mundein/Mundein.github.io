Services

I make small operations run organized like large operations should. I fix the little things that begin to snowball and turn into avalanches, before they become issues, and give companies both a sense of direction, and the organization to follow it. Here's what that looks like in practice:

Staffing & Schedules

Staffing plans and schedules built around how your operation actually runs. Coverage you can see coming, not gaps you find at shift start.

Dashboards & Reporting

One view of the numbers that matter: readiness, utilization, downtime, coverage. Built on your data, readable at a glance.
