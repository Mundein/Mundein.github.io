Straight Answers

What does it cost, total?

Three ways in. The Operations Review is $1,499 flat, with $750 to start and the balance when the report lands. Review + Build is $2,499 flat, half to start. Monthly Support is $750 a month, month to month, capped at 6 hours a month, and it is only offered after a Review or Build. No hourly billing and no surprise invoices.

Do we have to talk on the phone?

No. Most of it runs in writing, by text and email, so the findings and the plan sit in front of you before you pay. If you would rather talk it through first, Book a call on an evening that suits you.

When does the work happen?

Evenings and weekends. A Review runs about two weeks, and the walkthrough session gets booked at a time your operation is quiet.

What do you need from us?

Your schedules, your labor numbers as they stand, and an honest account of where it hurts. Messy is fine. Messy is the starting point for most operators.

Who owns the report and the dashboards?

You do. Everything is built in your systems and your accounts, and it stays with you even if we stop working together.
